3. WHAT IS ON DISK FOR YOU
=======================================================================
THE CELL, PARAMETRIC IN CONFIG.
  configs/cable_cell_v6_candidates.json   four declared cells, RC1 registered
  src/assembly_recovery/cable_cell_v6.py  the one place a declared cell becomes
                                          fixture overrides, CAD parts, or a case
  scripts/build_cell_cad.py               authors the CAD from that config
  scripts/screen_cell_v6.py               settles a cell and decides if it counts
  assets/cell_v6/                         8 STLs plus the source .FCStd

  Moving a clip is a config edit. Change `clips` in the candidate file, re-run
  build_cell_cad.py, re-run screen_cell_v6.py, and everything downstream follows.
  Nothing about the cell is hard-coded in a script. That is what your tool needs.

THE ASSET HASHES.
  evidence/cable_cell_cad_v6.json carries every STL's SHA-256 on LF-normalised
  bytes, and configs/cable_routing_v6.json copies them into `cell.asset_sha256`
  at freeze time. Every compiled scene's report carries them too, under
  fixture.cad_visuals. So a tool can prove it is showing the cell that was
  measured, and you should make it do that rather than trust the filename.

THE PER-STEP TRACES, FOR EVERY REQUEST AND NOT JUST A HANDFUL.
  artifacts/cable/routing-v6-s1/<request>/result.json -> `issued`, one record per
  decision, carrying:
    action, action_index, magnitude_m, abstained
    verdict            the filter's per-constraint judgement and headroom
    budget             headroom_at_decision_m, spent_by_this_motion_m,
                       spent_fraction, headroom_left_m, per constraint
    state_before       per-clip retention, min bend radius, peak anchor load
    state_after        the same, once the motion finished
    motion_completed   false means the job ended during it: CENSORED, not safe
    decision_boot_to_anchor_m / endpoint_boot_to_anchor_m
  and per request: clip_loss_attribution (which clip went, when, during which
  step), installed_clips, terminal_clips, seated, route_completed.

  artifacts/cable/routing-v6-s1/<request>/ledger.npz -> per-tick servo and native
  ledgers. The servo ledger's second-to-last channels now carry the RETAINED
  REQUIRED-CLIP COUNT, not a single clip boolean, so a five-clip route can be
  drawn losing clips one at a time.

  artifacts/cable/routing-v6-s1/<request>/scene.xml -> the compiled scene,
  referencing the CAD meshes by absolute path.

THE PICTURES.

4. WHAT I LEARNED THAT YOUR HANDOVER DOES NOT SAY
=======================================================================
FREECADCMD SILENTLY DOES NOTHING IF YOUR SCRIPT HAS A __main__ GUARD.
  `freecadcmd.exe script.py` runs the file under a module name of its own, so
  `if __name__ == "__main__":` never fires. The process exits 0, prints the
  banner, and writes nothing. The toolchain probe's verified pattern happens to
  have no guard, which is why it worked. Call main() directly. Also, __file__ is
  not bound, so resolve the repository root from the working directory.

THE FIVE-CLIP ROUTE IS A MUCH NARROWER OBJECT THAN A CLIP POSITION.
  The registered screen rejected 19 of 28 declared cells, and three of the four
  declared candidates survived at two service loops or fewer. A 132-cell pilot
  sweep came before that. Three things it measured, which are not obvious and
  cost real time:
    - The service-loop surplus must be parked on the leg AFTER the last clip. A
      6 mm surplus bowed into the 250 mm descent leg is a 28 mm lateral
      excursion, and it drags the cable out of every clip near the landing.
    - The first clip cannot sit at the landing point. A clip at along 80 mm is
      lost in almost every cell; the cable is still descending steeply there.
    - 22 mm clip pitch installs. 18 mm does not, reliably.
  If you let an engineer move a clip in your tool, re-run the SCREEN, not just
  the compile. A cell that compiles is not a cell that installs.

E0 REPEATS ARE NOT REPEATS.
  At E0 the estimate a supervisor reads IS the truth, exactly - it is asserted in
  the compiled scene. So a second perception draw at E0 is a byte-identical
  duplicate of the first. The composition study took four draws there and its E0
  metric resolution is optimistic by that factor; that is worth a sentence
  somewhere if you write about v5, and it is not a criticism of that block's
  verdicts, which do not turn on it. This block takes ONE draw at E0 and gets its
  E0 diversity from a registered five-point mounting-offset ladder instead,
  measured to flip outcomes inside a single group.

THE FILTER'S THRESHOLD IS A PROPERTY OF THE CELL, NOT OF THE CABLE.
  This is the thing the block is about and it is worth stating plainly in
  whatever you build. Measured before anything was scored:
    what a five-clip route physically admits   about 18 mm of commanded retreat
    what the shipped filter permits on it      58 mm, eleven of twelve candidates
  The filter refuses only the largest candidate, then issues one that spends 82
  per cent of the headroom it reports - and the route loses a clip on it. The
  threshold was fitted on a cell where 45 mm of routed cable could straighten;
  five clips pin it. The filter was NOT refitted and the action set was NOT
  rescaled to flatter it. Both would have answered a different question.

THE BUDGET DECAY IS DIRECTLY VISIBLE AND IS THE BEST THING TO DRAW.
  One conservative route, five steps, reported C1 headroom:
    0.0699  0.0666  0.0633  0.0600  0.0567 m
  3.3 mm spent per step, spent fraction rising 0.061 to 0.078. That is the
  composition mechanism - each accepted step spends slack the next is judged
  against - as a number per step rather than as a sentence. The fitter aggregates
  it under results[arm]["budget"]["per_step"].

A FOURTH ARM WAS CONSIDERED AND DELIBERATELY NOT REGISTERED.
  A supervisor capping the fraction of reported headroom one step may spend is
  the obvious engineering fix, and it uses only what the shipped layer already
  reports. It was left out because the registered twelve actions produce spent
  fractions of 0.061 and 0.819 at the first step with nothing in between: a
  fair-share cap of one fifth collapses onto the conservative arm, and any cap
  that separates them would have to be tuned. It needs its own action set and its
  own sizing. It is the best-motivated next block this project has, and the
  reasoning is recorded in the contract under pilot.considered_and_not_registered.

5. WHAT DID NOT CHANGE, AND MUST NOT
=======================================================================
  The cable: 23 segments at 20 mm, 0.46 m rest length, registered moduli and
    damping. The filter's C1 rule is a threshold on a boot-to-anchor DISTANCE
    fitted against exactly this cable. Lengthening it to buy board space would
    silently invalidate the threshold.
  The clip cross-section: 12 mm channel, 6 mm half-width, 5 mm lip gap, 12.5 mm
    lip height, -1 mm floor. Only the channel LENGTH was shortened, 30 mm to 12
    mm, and length does not enter cable_routes.clip_passages.
  The five-second settling deadline and the retention predicate.
  The twelve candidate actions, which are the perception study's.
  The single-clip code path: `clip` still reads as a one-element list, two
    registered perception contexts recompile to BYTE-IDENTICAL scene XML, and the
    settled centreline agrees to 0.0 m. Any change you make to build_fixture
    should keep passing that check - scripts do it in about ninety seconds.
  The contact defaults at cable_constrained_v2.py. Without them a 0.23 g capsule
    sinks through a 3 mm plate.
  No public page was published. The private one is unchanged at
    claude.ai/code/artifact/ef74d2ed-947f-4eab-a8bd-8674d4b917f4.

6. OPEN AND HONEST
=======================================================================
  The support is ONE cell at six service loops, crossed with two port mounts and
    five mounting offsets: 60 physical contexts, not ten held-out layouts. Thinner
    physical diversity than v5. Declared in the contract; say it in the tool.
  Three of the four declared candidate cells barely survived, so this cell is
    close to the edge of what installs at all. Worst installed lateral clearance
    is 0.41-0.53 mm, inside the 0.14-1.87 mm band the v4 reference layout installs
    at but not comfortably.
  The contract declares in advance that the filtered and unfiltered arms are
    expected to lose a clip at the FIRST step in most routes, which makes their
    per-step rates at steps 2 to 5 unresolvable. The five-step chain evidence
    comes from the conservative arm. That is a declared limitation, not a
    surprise to be explained away after the fact.
  Group RC1_l25 was fragile in the pilot - it lost a clip at every commanded
    retreat including 4 mm. It passed the registered screen and stays in the
    denominator. Worth looking at when the block is fitted.
