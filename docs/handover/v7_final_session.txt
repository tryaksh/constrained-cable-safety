1. WHAT YOU ARE SHOWING - FOUR BEATS, ALL MEASURED
=======================================================================
BEAT 1  ONE NUMBER IS ENOUGH, for a single move. 16,080 registered requests. A
        single scalar carrying a margin sized from the estimator's declared error
        is not beaten by any richer representation at any error level on any of
        three constraint shapes. The ~30,000-parameter network over the whole
        cable centreline is significantly WORSE on clip retention: gap 0.074,
        bootstrap interval excluding zero.

BEAT 2  IT MATTERS WHEN YOU CHAIN MOVES. 720 sequences. All 240 unfiltered
        sequences pulled the cable out of the clip, at every error level, against
        0.400-0.569 filtered. Universal, and therefore honest to show.

BEAT 3  AND THEN IT STOPS WORKING. 2,340 routes through a five-clip jig. The
        check bought NOTHING: with it and without it lose a clip at the same
        rate (gaps 0.000, +0.025, -0.008, all inside the 0.05 margin), both
        issuing exactly one move and losing a clip on it. The reason is
        measurable and was measured before scoring: the route tolerates about
        18 mm of pull-back and the check permits 58 mm, because its threshold is
        a property of the jig it was fitted on, not of the cable.

BEAT 4  WHAT DOES CARRY IS HOW MUCH YOU TAKE. Same check, same estimate, ranked
        by remaining headroom instead of "largest allowed": 20/60, 76/360 and
        9/360 routes completed against 0 of 780 for the greedy reading. This was
        an unplanned observation in study 2 and a REGISTERED prediction in study
        3, where it held at E0 and E2 and collapsed inside the margin at E4
        exactly as predicted. It is the one thing here that may be stated as a
        certified finding about appetite.

THE ONE-LINE TAKEAWAY, and it is honest: USE THE CHECK'S RANKING, NOT ITS
THRESHOLD. The ranking transfers to a jig it was never fitted on. The threshold
does not.

A detail worth drawing: of 1,144 clip losses, 1,017 were the clip at the TOP OF
THE RIDGE and 127 the clip nearest the plug. The other three never let go.

TWO THINGS THAT COST THE LAST SESSION REAL TIME.
  freecadcmd runs a script under a module name of its own, so an
  `if __name__ == "__main__"` guard never fires: the process prints its banner,
  exits 0, and writes nothing. Call main() directly. __file__ is not bound either.

  At E0 the estimate a supervisor reads IS the truth, asserted in the compiled
  scene, so a second perception draw at E0 is a byte-identical duplicate. v5 took
  four there and its E0 resolution is optimistic by that factor - worth a sentence
  if you write about v5, and not a criticism of its verdicts, which do not turn on
  it. v6 takes one draw at E0 and gets its E0 diversity from a registered
  five-point mounting-offset ladder instead.
